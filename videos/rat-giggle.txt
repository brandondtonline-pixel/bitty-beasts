Rats giggle when you tickle them! 🐀💕

When scientists tickled rats, the rats let out happy chirps at around 50 kHz. That's far too high for us to hear: human hearing tops out around 20 kHz. Their most ticklish spot is the belly (not the back or tail). Tickled rats do little "joy jumps" and chase the tickling hand to ask for more, but anxious rats, under bright lights or up on a high platform, giggle much less.

In a later study, the same lab even taught rats to play hide-and-seek with people, with tickles as the only prize.

Source: Ishiyama & Brecht 2016, Science, via Science News https://www.sciencenews.org/article/giggling-rats-help-reveal-how-brain-creates-joy and National Geographic https://www.nationalgeographic.com/animals/article/rats-tickling-brains-moods · Reinhold et al. 2019, Science (hide-and-seek), https://awionline.org/awi-quarterly/fall-2019/rats-enjoy-playing-hide-and-seek/
