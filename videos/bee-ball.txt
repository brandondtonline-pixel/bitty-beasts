Bumblebees play! 🐝⚽

In a 2022 lab study, 45 bumblebees had a clear path to sugar and a side area with little wooden balls. They kept detouring to roll the balls, even though there was no food there. Each bee rolled a ball anywhere from 1 to 117 times!

Later, given two coloured rooms with no balls in either, bees preferred the colour of the room where they'd played. Young bees played the most, and males rolled each ball for longer. It's the first time scientists have seen object play in an insect.

Source: Galpayage Dona et al. 2022, "Do bumble bees play?", Animal Behaviour 194, 239–251 · https://www.oulu.fi/en/news/first-ever-study-shows-bumble-bees-play
