Bumblebees play! 🐝⚽

In a 2022 experiment, 45 bumblebees could fly straight to a sugar feeder or take a detour through an area with little wooden balls. Many took the detour and rolled the balls around again and again, with no food or reward for doing it. One bee rolled a ball 117 times! Younger bees played the most. It's the first time scientists have seen object play in an insect.

Bees can learn ball tricks too: in a 2017 study, bees watched a trained bee roll a ball into a goal for a sugary treat, then did it themselves, often using a closer ball to make it easier.

Source: Galpayage Dona et al. 2022, Animal Behaviour, "Do bumble bees play?" https://www.oulu.fi/en/news/first-ever-study-shows-bumble-bees-play · Loukola et al. 2017, Science, https://www.sciencenews.org/article/score-bumblebees-football-insect-social-learning
