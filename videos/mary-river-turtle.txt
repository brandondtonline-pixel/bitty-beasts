Meet the Mary River turtle, Australia's punk turtle. 🐢💚

That green mohawk is algae. It grows on the turtle's head and shell because it spends so long underwater, and it can stay under for up to about two and a half days thanks to gill-like structures in its tail that take in oxygen from the water. Yes, it breathes through its butt.

In the 1960s and '70s, hatchlings were sold in Australian pet shops as "penny turtles", years before scientists formally named the species in 1994. It can live a very long time but doesn't start breeding until it's about 20, and today it's endangered, with volunteers guarding its riverbank nests from predators.
