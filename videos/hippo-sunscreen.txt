Hippos make their own sunscreen! 🦛☀️

A hippo's famous "blood sweat" is neither blood nor sweat. It oozes out of special skin glands as a clear fluid and turns red-orange within minutes. Scientists in Japan swabbed it from the faces and backs of two zoo hippos and found two pigments, a red one and an orange one, that both soak up ultraviolet light like a natural sunscreen. The red pigment also slows the growth of disease-causing bacteria, which may help explain why hippo wounds rarely get infected. And hippos make more of it on land than in the water.

Source: Saikawa et al. 2004, Nature 429:363, via Nature News https://www.nature.com/news/2004/040524/full/news040524-7.html · Journal of Experimental Biology, https://cob.silverchair.com/jeb/article/207/19/v/14939/HIPPOS-LEAD-THE-WAY-IN-MULTIPURPOSE-SUN-SCREEN
