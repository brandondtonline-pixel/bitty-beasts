Sea otter pups can't sink! 🦦☁️

Newborn pups have such thick, fluffy baby fur that they're too buoyant to dive. Mom grooms her pup constantly, fluffing tiny air bubbles into its fur, and when she heads down for food she just leaves her baby bobbing on the surface like a little cork. Pups start diving for themselves at about two months old.

Unlike most marine mammals, sea otters have no blubber. Instead they have the densest fur of any mammal: more than a million hairs per square inch (you've probably got under 100,000 on your whole head). Adults can dive to 600 feet and stay down for 6–7 minutes, and they eat about a quarter of their body weight every day.
